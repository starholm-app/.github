Starholm is a library of emergency guides and manuals with a private AI assistant, kept on your own computer. It works with no internet and no phone signal, and nothing you ask leaves your computer.

The app is free and open source. A one-time upgrade adds the illustrated guides, offline maps, a family plan and a cleaned library.
